# Weekly expenses review

Analyze the complete week against the preceding weeks and the matching profile basis.

- Distinguish an isolated purchase, a recurring monthly or yearly cost, a sustained level change, and a short burst such as travel.
- Compare category and label changes with their matching deterministic profile. Do not compare a weekly total with a monthly average.
- Prefer the classification correlation over merchant history when the merchant is a payment intermediary.
- Merge exact opposing movements into one concise possible offset or cash-recomposition finding.
- For a material acquisition, inspect nearby smaller transactions available
  through the report tools when they clarify its complete context, even when
  each is small relative to the main payment.
- Keep at most a handful of findings. A quiet week has an empty findings
  container, without a summary total or a “nothing to report” message.
